# Dictation, Magic and the iOS companion

On Mac, dictate into a floating draft near the caret, apply Magic on demand, then insert or discard the draft. On iPhone, use the keyboard preview for dictation and Magic. The iOS companion has History, Meetings and Settings tabs. See [Dictation and Magic on Mac](mac-field-magic.md) for the desktop controls.

## Runtime ownership

`MicrophoneHost` owns the microphone lifecycle. `KeyboardSession` follows keyboard presence and connects speech to `DictationEngine`; `SpeechModeSink` routes ordinary speech to `DictationPipeline` and Magic speech to `InstructionSession`. Provider connections and credentials remain in the host app.

`Room` orders ASR envelopes, publishes partial and final speech, tracks consumer acknowledgements during transcription draining and writes redacted session events. It has no mutable composition document, document tools, writer conversation, provisional edits or hint binder. Its historical channel and participant values remain decodable for old logs; document publication is rejected. `Word` remains the shared ASR word contract, including confidence and language.

`LegatoAgent` retains cleanup, recent-take context, `InstructionRunner`, `ModelChoice` and the confidence-mark stripping helper. Bundled prompts cover line cleanup, whole-take cleanup and field instructions. Composition modes and document-writing prompts are removed. `LegatoComposerDoc` contains `Word`. `LegatoComposerUI` owns the shared countdown rim and Magic preview effects used by iPhone and Mac.

## Keyboard delivery and Magic

Typed-line dictation uses `DictationLink`; live dictation and revisions use `LiveDictationLink`. `DictationStatusLink` carries microphone route, errors, tail, take progress, countdown and notices, with Stop, Retry and Insert as-is controls. It carries no document snapshot or animation history. Magic keeps its separate instruction presentation and intent protocol.

The keyboard mic starts and stops ordinary dictation. The Magic key supports tap-to-latch and hold-to-talk; speech while Magic is active becomes an instruction about readable text in the field. Turning Magic off runs the instruction after its last words arrive. The keyboard shows the instruction, reading and editing progress, and result actions. `InstructionRunner` edits a verified selection or field scope; `FieldQueue` owns application and confirmation. Dictation cleanup and Magic retain separate model settings.

## History and recovery

Existing History entries remain available. Dictation and Magic use the shared history store; failed or unconfirmed field edits retain recoverable text through their existing recovery paths. The retirement does not clear history, vocabulary, credentials or old session files.

`ComposerHistoryBackfill` remains a bounded, one-time import for historical microphone sessions. It considers up to the 50 newest session directories, requires the recorded microphone source, a terminal `done` event and nonempty `final.md`, and skips existing entries. Recovered entries retain the `composer.recovered` engine identifier. Current ASR Rooms no longer write composition drafts to `final.md`.

Old composer UAT reports, session logs and spike artifacts describe the builds named in those records. They do not establish acceptance of the current field workflow. Local package tests, built-app checks, agent UAT, physical-device checks and paid provider tests remain separate evidence.

## Meetings (Mac)

A meeting records the microphone and the audio the Mac plays (`SystemAudioSource`, a Core Audio process tap) as one mix on one Soniox `stt-rt-v6` stream with speaker diarization (`MeetingSession`, `SonioxMeetingEngine`; see the headers of `MeetingServices` and `MeetingSession` for reconnects, gaps and the 300-minute rollover). Separate mic, system and mix audio are written to the meeting's folder (`Application Support/Legato/Meetings/<id>/`), later converted to m4a. Speakers are "Speaker N" until renamed; a name already in use merges the two people, in the transcript and in the written summaries. After stop, Claude (the user's key, `MeetingSummarizer`) writes the summary unless `MeetingSettings.summarizeAutomatically` is off; a failure is kept in `meeting.summaryFailure` and shown with Try again. A meeting still titled by its date (`titleIsAutomatic`) gets a short Claude title (`MeetingTitle`, a separate request) after its first summary; editing the title clears the flag. A stopped meeting under 60 s or without transcript can be discarded in one click (`Meeting.isDiscardable`).

Settings, Meetings has two opt-in cards, both off by default. **Calls** (`MeetingSettings.callDetection`: Off, Ask, Record) runs `CallDetectionController`, which polls the Core Audio process list once a second only while the mode is not Off. `CallDetectionPolicy` (pure, in `LegatoMac`) decides: a call is another process, not Legato by pid or by the `de.gruenwald.legato` bundle prefix, with mic and speakers both running; it begins after 3 s without a break, once per call (dismissing the prompt does not bring it back), and never during dictation, Magic or a meeting (a call seen during a meeting counts as handled). Ask shows `CallPromptController`, a non-activating card in the island's look that names the app and goes away after 20 s; Record starts the meeting by itself and marks it `MeetingController.startedAutomatically`. The call is over when its process has had neither mic nor speakers for 10 s (a mute that closes the mic does not end it), and then only a meeting that started automatically stops; one the user started, from the card too, never does. **Calendar** (`MeetingSettings.calendarTitles`) asks for full calendar access with a button (`CalendarAccess`, EventKit stays in the app target; `NSCalendarsFullAccessUsageDescription` and the calendars entitlement are in `project.yml`). When a meeting starts, a lookup beside the recording picks the event on now or starting within 5 minutes, not all-day, preferring one with attendees, then the closest start (`CalendarEventChoice`, plain values). `MeetingServices.applyCalendarEvent` gives a meeting still titled by its date that title and `Meeting.attendees` (the user left out) and clears `titleIsAutomatic`, so the calendar outranks Claude's title and a title the user typed stays. A failed or empty lookup is silent. The attendees appear in the speaker rename popover as names to pick, except names already in the meeting.

Dictation and Magic are refused while a meeting records (`MeetingServices.dictationRefusal`, shown on the recording island), and a meeting is refused while either runs. A missing Soniox key fails the start before anything is recorded; a missing Claude key keeps the meeting and shows the summary error. If Soniox drops, the audio keeps recording and the transcript marks the gap. A meeting cut off by a quit comes back as interrupted with its audio (`recover()` at launch).

The Meetings window (`MeetingsWindow`, opened from the menu-bar menu) has three columns: the sidebar (`MeetingSidebar`: All Meetings, Client then Project folders, tags; the only code that knows the folder model), the day-grouped list with search over title, filing, summary and transcript, and the detail. The detail has an editable title, client, project and tags (with the values other meetings use as suggestions, editable while recording), a Summary and a Transcript tab (speaker chips rename people; gaps are marked; the live meeting follows its newest line), the audio player, and Copy Summary, Copy Transcript, Share (Markdown) and Delete. Recording state sits on the notch island (`MeetingIsland`: elapsed time, Stop, and warnings such as a silent system-audio tap or a transcription outage). Debug builds can draw the window from sample meetings without a session, a key or the network: `-LegatoMeetingsRoot <empty dir> -LegatoMeetingsShots <dir>` (`MeetingsPreview`) writes a PNG per state and quits. The first real system-audio capture needs the System Audio Recording permission.

### Meetings from agents and scripts

`legato` (`LegatoMeetingsCLI`; `Sources/legato` is the executable) reads the meetings folder, read-only: it never writes in it, a missing folder is an empty result, and a transcript line the app is still writing is skipped. `legato meetings list|show <id>|search <words>` take `--client`, `--project`, `--tag` (repeatable), `--root <dir>` and `--json` (Markdown/plain text otherwise); `show` accepts any unambiguous start of an id. `legato mcp` serves `list_meetings`, `show_meeting` and `search_meetings` as an MCP server on stdio (newline-delimited JSON-RPC; stdout is protocol only, it exits when stdin closes). Search is `MeetingStore.matches(_:meeting:)`, shared with the Meetings window: title, filing, speakers, summaries, notes and transcript. `legato meetings speakers --replay <id|folder>` replays the after-meeting speaker check without saving anything and prints counts only ([speakers-replay.md](../Sources/LegatoMeetingsCLI/speakers-replay.md)).

The Mac app bundles the binary as `Legato.app/Contents/Helpers/legato` (the `LegatoCLI` tool target in `Apps/macOS/project.yml`, copied and signed with the app; not `Contents/MacOS`, where `legato` and `Legato` are one name on a case-insensitive disk). `MeetingToolsSettings` links it to `~/.local/bin/legato` and copies the MCP configuration with the bundled path. From a checkout: `swift run legato meetings list`.

## Historical session inspection

Historical `sessions/<UUID>/` directories may contain `room.jsonl`, `final.md` and recorded audio. Room envelopes include sequence, channel, participant, origin/publication times and payload; old document events may also include revision and causality fields. Read these records as historical artifacts rather than current writer behavior.

The [device harness guide](../scripts/device/README.md) documents how to pull app-container files. `SessionLog` redacts supplied secrets, sensitive field names and bearer/key patterns; speech and other personal content remain sensitive. The retired composer debug seed, edit, insert and clear commands are unavailable. Use the current dictation and Magic fixture commands for offline field testing.

# Anchored correction UI — 2026-10-30

Word taps and deliberate press-slide quotes share one selection, highlight, measured-glyph popup and speech-target route in the native iOS and Mac apps. The popup has a subtle “Speak a correction” heading and displays captured speech rather than repeating the quote. The wand submits immediately; after 500 ms of quiet the host schedules a one-second perimeter glow and autosend. New speech interrupts that host countdown. Popup X discards the captured instruction without changing the draft.

The popup sits entirely above or below the visible marked extent, pointing to its first or last selected glyph row. It fits the viewport horizontally. When neither side fits, the composer reserves real text-free space and scrolls the selection into its remaining text viewport; it does not overlay the highlight or resize the iOS keyboard. Its content can scroll within the available height. Both native appearances use the same material surface, selection tint and glyph-change renderer.
